## 阶段 A: 调候/流年提质 (P0)

**目标**: 压低 `wrong_tiaohou` / `wrong_liunian`, val 准确率较 35% 有明显提升。

### A1. 错题归因复核

1. 读取 `contest8_val_geju_special_regression.json` 中 wrong_tiaohou / wrong_liunian 题目列表
2. 逐题核对: judgement 是否命中应有 ruleId、targetYear 是否解析、activeDayun 是否正确

**涉及文件**:
- `code/backend/app/benchmark/contest8_benchmark_meta.py`
- `code/backend/app/core/knowledge/luck_chart.py`
- `code/backend/app/core/judgement/judges.py` (TiaohouJudge, SuiYunJudge)
- `code/backend/app/core/agent/prompts_contest.py`

### A2. 规则与 prompt 补丁

按归因结果选择一项或多项:

- 补《穷通宝鉴》调候 lookupKey 缺口 (extract + manifest 编译)
- 补 liunian event 节点 (sanming / suiyun 专题)
- 强化 `build_liunian_prompt_block` / `_format_judgement_block` 调候与流年段落

### A3. 验证 (不跑全量 40 题)


**验收标准**:
- 8题: coverageRate=1.0, caseOverreach=0, primary>=16
- 8题 LLM: 准确率 > 12.5% (至少多对 2 题) 或 wrong_tiaohou/liunian 标签减少
- 20题: coverageRate>=0.95, caseOverreach<=1

**产出**: `继续补全/val_baseline_20_rag.json`, `继续补全/contest8_val_sample_llm.json`
